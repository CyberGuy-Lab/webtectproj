My Story

I'm Ebenezer Adarkwa Yiadom, known as Elshadai. A Level 200 Cybersecurity and Digital Forensics student at Akenten Appiah-Menka University of Skills Training and Entrepreneurial Development, I'm driven by a deep passion to protect digital systems and empower communities across Ghana.

As founder of the ElshadaiImpact Foundation, I work to teach technology skills to children in Bekwai and beyond — creating opportunity and digital literacy for the next generation. I also envision CyberNexus, a platform to nurture young tech talent and expand cybersecurity awareness nationwide.

Guided by values of love, integrity, innovation, and compassion, my mission is to leave a lasting impact on everyone I meet and contribute meaningfully to Ghana's digital future.
